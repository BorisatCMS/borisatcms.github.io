My strong suit is editing videos in various formats, with a strong focus on sound: from quick edits set to music to animations made in After Effects and full Sound Design, which I put together in Premiere Pro or Audition, depending on the complexity of the project.

And sound determines more than 50% of the final product.

Imagine a new high-end phone with a beautiful screen. But during a phone call, the microphone picks up the voice:

1. Either with noise;
2. Or not at all.

The person on the other end keeps asking you to repeat yourself.
The screen is still the same, but the phone fails at its single most important job.

If the image - the phone's screen - answers the question "what's happening?", then the sound answers the question "what should we pay attention to, and how pleasant is it to watch the video?".
